Start your own practice

Thinking about opening your own practice? Start with a list, then work it in order. Three stages, no giant leap.

01 — Form

Get the shape right

Decide what you will offer, what you will charge, and what the first months need to cover. A simple pricing plan and a monthly money picture come before almost everything else.

02 — Build

Set up the back office

Everyday admin, written down once: office procedures, patient messages, schedules, and a finance tracker. This is the pile nobody warns you about, sorted before opening day.

03 — Launch

Open with a checklist

An opening-week checklist, a simple website, and a workflow for week one, so opening day is a list you follow instead of a scramble you survive.

What Practice Foundry does not do: credentialing, payer enrollment, EHR setup, or legal and regulatory filings. Those stay with you and your licensed professionals. Practice Foundry covers the planning, pricing, paperwork, and everyday systems around them.

This is general, practical help. It is tailored to how you already work, and you stay in charge of every decision and final draft.
