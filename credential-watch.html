CredentialWatch by Practice Foundry

No license should expire by surprise.

CredentialWatch keeps a watch on staff credential expiry dates and reminds the right people before anything lapses. Built for small practices that still track renewals on a spreadsheet and hope for the best.

What it watches

  • State medical and professional licenses
  • DEA registrations
  • Board certifications
  • Malpractice policy renewals
  • BLS and ACLS cards
  • CME progress, logged as you earn it

Dates are entered by your team. CredentialWatch counts down the days and does the remembering.

How it works

01

Add people and dates

Add each staff member, the credential, and the expiry date. That is the whole setup.

02

CredentialWatch watches

One dashboard sorts everything into fine, coming due, and urgent. Reminders go to the staff member and the manager at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days.

03

Renew, manager confirms

The staff member enters the new date after renewing. The manager approves it, and the reminders reset. Nothing closes on someone's word alone.

Who it is for

Small practices and clinics with roughly 5 to 50 staff — family medicine, dental, therapy, behavioral health, med spas, and similar teams where one expired credential means someone cannot work. If one person tracks every renewal from memory and a spreadsheet, this is built for you.

What CredentialWatch does not do: it does not file credentialing or payer enrollment applications, and it stores no patient data. It is a reminder tool. Each practice stays responsible for completing and verifying its own renewals.
